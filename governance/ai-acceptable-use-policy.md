# AI Acceptable Use Policy

> **M365 Copilot Security Lab**
>
> This document is a fictional educational policy created for the
> M365 Copilot Security Lab. It does not represent the policy of any
> real organization and contains no production or employer data.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for the secure, responsible, and
productive use of Microsoft Copilot and other approved generative AI
capabilities within a fictional Microsoft 365 environment.

AI tools are intended to assist users with their work. They do not
replace human judgment, established security controls, or individual
responsibility for the final work product.

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Promote responsible use of generative AI.
- Protect sensitive and confidential information.
- Establish expectations for reviewing AI-generated content.
- Define acceptable and prohibited uses.
- Encourage appropriate reporting of AI-related security concerns.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to fictional users participating in the M365
Copilot Security Lab.

It covers the use of Microsoft Copilot for activities including:

- Entering prompts
- Uploading or referencing content
- Generating text or other content
- Summarizing information
- Analyzing information
- Drafting communications
- Creating technical or business content

This lab does not connect to a production Microsoft 365 environment.

---

## 3. Key Definitions

**Copilot**

Microsoft-provided generative AI capabilities used within the scope
of this fictional lab.

**Prompt**

A question, instruction, or piece of information provided to an AI
system.

**AI-Generated Output**

Content generated in response to a user's prompt, including text,
summaries, recommendations, formulas, code, or other generated
material.

**Sensitive Information**

Information that requires additional protection because of its
confidential, personal, financial, legal, security, contractual,
or other sensitive nature.

**Human Review**

The process of evaluating AI-generated content for accuracy,
appropriateness, completeness, security, and suitability before use.

---

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### IT and Security

In a fictional organizational deployment, IT and security personnel
would be responsible for supporting appropriate identity, access,
security, and governance controls surrounding approved AI services.

Responsibilities may include:

- Managing approved AI services and access.
- Establishing security requirements.
- Maintaining guidance for users.
- Supporting security incident reporting.
- Reviewing AI-related security risks.

### Users

Users are responsible for:

- Using only authorized AI tools and accounts.
- Protecting sensitive information.
- Following applicable access controls.
- Reviewing AI-generated content before relying on it.
- Reporting suspected security or data-handling issues.
- Remaining accountable for work completed with AI assistance.

---

## 5. Acceptable Use

Appropriate AI-assisted activities may include:

- Drafting routine communications.
- Summarizing approved information.
- Brainstorming ideas.
- Developing outlines.
- Creating training materials.
- Drafting process documentation.
- Organizing information.
- Assisting with formulas or scripts.
- Improving the clarity or structure of existing content.

Use of AI should support legitimate and authorized activities.

---

## 6. Data Handling

Users should consider the sensitivity of information before providing
it to an AI system.

Users should:

- Provide only the information necessary for the task.
- Avoid providing credentials or authentication secrets.
- Avoid unnecessary personal or confidential information.
- Follow applicable information classification requirements.
- Confirm that information is appropriate for the AI service being used.
- Maintain existing access restrictions when sharing generated content.

Access to information does not automatically mean that information
should be included in an AI prompt.

When uncertain about the sensitivity of information, users should
seek appropriate guidance before using it.

---

## 7. Output Validation

AI-generated content may contain incorrect, incomplete, misleading,
outdated, or fabricated information.

Users should review generated content before relying on it.

Review should include, as appropriate:

- Checking factual claims.
- Reviewing calculations.
- Verifying references or citations.
- Checking for unintended sensitive information.
- Reviewing tone and appropriateness.
- Identifying unsupported conclusions.
- Testing generated code or technical instructions in an appropriate
  non-production environment.

AI-generated content should not be considered verified solely because
it appears confident or professionally written.

---

## 8. Prohibited Use

Users should not use AI systems to:

- Enter passwords, private keys, access tokens, recovery codes, or
  authentication secrets.
- Circumvent access restrictions or security controls.
- Access information they are not authorized to access.
- Disclose restricted information to unauthorized recipients.
- Generate malicious code or content intended to compromise systems.
- Create deceptive impersonations.
- Intentionally generate harmful or unlawful content.
- Bypass licensing or usage restrictions.
- Misrepresent unverified AI-generated information as verified fact.

AI should not independently make high-impact decisions affecting
individuals or organizational security.

---

## 9. Human Accountability

Users remain responsible for work created with AI assistance.

AI-generated content should receive an appropriate level of human
review before it is:

- Shared
- Published
- Used for decision-making
- Used in technical implementation
- Presented as factual information
- Used as an official business record

The level of review should increase when the potential impact of an
error is higher.

---

## 10. Security Incident Reporting

Potential AI-related security concerns should be reported through the
organization's established security or IT reporting process.

Examples may include:

- Accidental disclosure of sensitive information
- Unexpected access to information
- Exposure of authentication credentials
- Suspected misuse of an AI system
- Unexpected AI behavior with potential security impact

Users should preserve relevant information needed for an authorized
security review and should not conduct their own investigation unless
authorized.

---

## 11. Training and Awareness

Users should receive appropriate guidance before using organizational
AI tools.

Training should include:

- Responsible AI use
- Prompting practices
- Data handling
- Security awareness
- Human review requirements
- Appropriate use cases
- Incident reporting

AI security awareness should continue as technologies, organizational
requirements, and risks evolve.

---

## 12. Quick Reference: Before Using Copilot

Before submitting information to Copilot, ask:

- [ ] Am I authorized to use this information?
- [ ] Am I using an approved AI service?
- [ ] Does my prompt contain sensitive information?
- [ ] Can unnecessary information be removed?
- [ ] Does this task require additional human review?
- [ ] Can I verify the generated response?
- [ ] Am I handling the resulting content appropriately?
